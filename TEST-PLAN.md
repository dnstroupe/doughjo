# Participant test plan

Status: not conducted. Use fictional details. Obtain consent before recording.

1. Find a donut inspired by a character you know. Explain its flavor and price.
2. Build a box of six with at least one repeated flavor.
3. Move one donut, remove another, and recover it with Undo.
4. Swap a flavor. Observe whether it is clear that the original stays until a replacement is chosen.
5. Switch to twelve, add another donut, then return to six. Observe understanding of the removal warning.
6. Make Limitless Glaze unavailable using the demo control. Recover without help.
7. Use the flavor finder and explain why those results appear.
8. Explain the full price before checkout.
9. Trigger a failed demo attempt. Ask whether an order exists and what should happen next.
10. Repeat key actions with keyboard and preferred assistive technology.

Record task completion, assistance, hesitation, errors, recovery, exact participant comments, device, browser, and voluntarily shared assistive technology. Separate observations from interpretations. Prioritize blockers, revise, and retest. Do not claim population results from a small formative sample.

Additional tasks for the expanded shop: review a suggested squad and change one flavor; add two different drinks, remove one and Undo; explain whether drinks occupy box slots; compare the menu image with the box image; complete the reveal with motion off.
